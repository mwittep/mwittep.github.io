About

Integrative computational analyses across the central dogma

Antibiotic resistance is rising while the discovery of new compounds is slowing down. Finding new ways to control microbes means understanding the mechanisms behind what they do — and no single layer of data explains that on its own.

My doctoral research, in the Integrative Transcriptomics group at the University of Tübingen under Prof. Dr. Kay Nieselt, focused on the development of computational methods that work across the central dogma: genome, transcriptome, and the structures they encode.

In practice that meant two things. Building integrative pipelines that combine data sources rather than analysing them in isolation — and putting them behind interfaces that biologists can use without first resolving a dependency tree.

Before the PhD I completed my BSc and MSc in bioinformatics in Tübingen, having moved from Guayaquil, Ecuador was funded by the Cluster of Excellence Controlling Microbes to Fight Infections. I currently work with the Brochado Lab .

Evidente

2022

SNPs in their evolutionary context

Visual analytics tool genome

SNP-based phylogenetic trees are usually read as trees alone, leaving the underlying genomic variation unexplored. Evidente classifies SNPs by clade-specificity using CLASSICO and puts them next to the tree, sample metadata, and GO-term enrichment in a single interface — so genome-wide variation can be interpreted evolutionarily to generate functional and phenotypic hypotheses. Applied to Mycobacterium leprae and Treponema pallidum datasets.

TSSpredator-Web

2026

Transcription start sites, predicted and explored

Web application genome transcriptome

TSSpredator identifies and classifies transcription start sites from genome-wide transcriptomic data, but running it and making sense of the output took real computational skill. TSSpredator-Web wraps the prediction in a browser interface and adds an integrated genome browser, so genome-wide TSS maps can be explored together with the underlying genomic data across conditions. Demonstrated on E. coli under three antibiotic treatments.

Accepted for publication in PLOS ONE (February 2026)

TSS-Captur

2024

Characterizing the transcripts nobody classified

Nextflow pipeline & web app genome transcriptome

TSS prediction leaves behind a pile of unclassified start sites that most workflows simply drop. TSS-Captur takes those orphans and reconstructs what they actually transcribe: sequence classification into coding or non-coding, terminator prediction, secondary structure, and promoter motif analysis — surfacing previously overlooked non-coding RNAs and small protein-coding genes. Results come back as an interactive report.

The metallophore landscape of Staphylococcus

2025

A genus-wide map of who can make, and who can steal, iron

Computational study genome

Bacteria secrete metallophores to scavenge scarce metals — and because those molecules work outside the cell, neighbours with the right receptor can help themselves. What S. aureus does is well documented; the rest of the genus was not. This is the first systematic genus-wide characterization, covering 74 species and over 1,900 strains from the GTDB. Every species carries the staphyloferrin A locus, but only 17 can make staphyloferrin B, and uncharacterized biosynthetic clusters turned up along the way.
